Support and contact

  • Questions or problems with the app: support@revivo.training
  • Your data (access, correction, export, deletion, withdrawing consent): most of it is in the app, in Settings → Privacy. For the rest, write to privacy@revivo.training. We answer within 30 days.
  • Deleting your account: see the deletion page.
  • Report abuse or illegal content: in the app, long-press the athlete's name and choose "Report". For teams, groups, or if you have no account, write to moderare@revivo.training with what you saw and where.
  • Parents: the link in the consent email lets you change or withdraw consent at any time. If you lost it, write to privacy@revivo.training.
  • Security issues: security@revivo.training (see security.txt).
  • Phone: +40 731 892 420

You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro).
